Privacy Policy

The short version
  • Better Charting does not collect, store, or share any of your data.
  • Voice is turned into text on your device only, using Apple’s on-device speech recognition.
  • No audio or text is ever sent to the developer or to any server.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • Nothing is saved. Closing the app or tapping Clear erases the statement.

Who we are

Better Charting is a small app made by Katie Escobar, a home health RN. It helps nurses build homebound statements for Medicare home health documentation. In this policy, “we” means the developer of Better Charting.

What the app does with your words

When you use voice, the app listens through your microphone and turns your speech into text using Apple’s on-device speech recognition (Apple’s SpeechAnalyzer/DictationTranscriber, or SFSpeechRecognizer set to require on-device recognition). The app does not use server-based speech recognition.

If on-device recognition isn’t available on your device or for your language, the app turns voice off instead of sending your speech anywhere. You can still type your answers.

The text you dictate or type is held only in the app’s memory while you are working. It is used for one thing: building your homebound statement on screen.

What we collect

Nothing. Better Charting does not collect, receive, store, sell, or share any personal information or patient information. Specifically:

  • No audio or text is sent to the developer or to any server.
  • There are no user accounts or sign-ins.
  • There is no analytics, crash-reporting SDK, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • The developer never sees or receives any patient information.

Nothing is saved

The app does not save statements, answers, or recordings. When you close the app or tap Clear, the statement is erased. Audio is not recorded to a file.

Copying to your EMR

When you tap Copy, the statement is placed on your device’s clipboard so you can paste it into your EMR yourself. The clipboard is managed by Apple’s operating system, not by Better Charting. The text stays on the clipboard until you copy something else. If Universal Clipboard (Handoff) is turned on, Apple may make the clipboard available on your other Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Please follow your agency’s policy for handling copied patient information.

Apple’s speech model download

The first time you use voice, your iPhone, iPad, or Mac may download Apple’s on-device speech model through the operating system. This download comes from Apple and contains no user data. It is handled by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone – so the app can hear your answers.
  • Speech Recognition – so your device can turn your speech into text.

Both are optional. If you say no, you can still type. You can change these any time in your device’s Settings (or System Settings on Mac) under Privacy & Security.

Your responsibilities

Better Charting is designed so that no patient information leaves your device. It is not HIPAA certified, and no app can make a workflow compliant on its own. You are responsible for following your agency’s policies on devices, patient information, and documentation, and for reviewing every statement before you sign it. Use only findings you have assessed. Please dictate while parked, never while driving.

Children

Better Charting is a professional tool for clinicians and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever begins handling data differently, this page will say so clearly before that change takes effect.
