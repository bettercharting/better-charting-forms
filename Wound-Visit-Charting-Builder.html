Golden rule: Describe what you see, not what you assume. Measurements, tissue type, drainage, pain, and periwound condition at every visit — not just at SOC. If the wound changed, say what changed. If it didn't, say it didn't.

📏 Measurements

▼
Measure every visit. Length = head-to-toe (12→6 o'clock). Width = side-to-side (9→3 o'clock). Depth = deepest point with cotton swab. Tunneling/undermining: direction + cm. Trends justify skilled need.

🩸 Wound Bed

▼
Tissue types: Granulation = red/beefy (healing). Slough = yellow/tan/gray (non-viable). Eschar = black/brown necrotic (never debride without orders). Note % of each. Infection: document what you assessed, not just "infected."

🩹 Periwound Skin

▼
Check: Is periwound skin intact, macerated, denuded, or erythematous? Measure erythema in cm from wound edge. Is it spreading? Track the trend from last visit.

💧 Drainage & Odor

▼
Be consistent. Use the same terms every visit. Note changes from last visit — trend matters. If NPWT, document canister mL and input vs. output.

🤕 Pain Assessment

▼
Assess before, during, and after. One number is not enough. Distinguish wound pain from baseline chronic pain. Did pre-medication help? Did you try non-drug options?

🧤 Dressing Change

▼
Count in, count out. Every NPWT visit. If the count doesn't match, do not leave without resolving it. Document old dressing condition, solution + volume, layers applied, and patient tolerance.

📚 Patient & Caregiver Teaching

▼
Name the topic, method, and response. "Taught wound care" is not skilled documentation. Did the caregiver return-demonstrate? What barriers were identified?

📞 Escalation

▼
When to call: spreading erythema, purulent drainage, new foul odor, fever ≥100.4°F, uncontrolled pain, wound stalled or increasing, maceration spreading, no improvement after 2 weeks. If you called — document time, who, what was discussed, and orders received.

🏥 Skilled Rationale

▼
Every wound visit note must answer: why does this wound require a skilled nurse? State what the nurse did that the patient/caregiver cannot do safely, what complication is being prevented, and what progress is being tracked.

📅 Next Visit Plan

▼
State the plan: what will you assess next visit, what teaching will you reinforce, and what complication are you watching for?
