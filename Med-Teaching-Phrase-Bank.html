💊 Anticoagulants

▼
Teaching cues:
  • Purpose: prevents blood clots, does not "thin" blood — changes how it clots
  • Bleeding precautions: watch for unusual bruising, nosebleeds, blood in urine/stool, prolonged bleeding from cuts, black tarry stools
  • Warfarin: dietary vitamin K consistency, INR monitoring, same dose same time daily
  • DOACs (Eliquis, Xarelto): no regular lab monitoring, take consistently, stop before procedures as ordered
  • When to call MD: unusual bleeding, fall with head injury, missed doses

💊 Antibiotics

▼
Teaching cues:
  • Complete the full course — do not stop even if feeling better
  • Take with food or on empty stomach as directed
  • Watch for: rash, hives, difficulty breathing (call 911), severe diarrhea, GI upset
  • Don't take leftover antibiotics for new symptoms without MD order
  • When to call MD: worsening symptoms, no improvement after 48–72 hours, severe diarrhea

💊 Pain Meds / Opioids

▼
Teaching cues:
  • Dosing: exact schedule, maximum daily dose, not to double up if a dose is missed
  • Side effects: sedation, dizziness, fall risk, constipation, nausea
  • Constipation prevention: hydration, fiber, stool softener or laxative as ordered
  • Fall risk: take with food, avoid getting up quickly, use nightlights
  • Safe storage: keep away from children/others, dispose of unused meds properly
  • When to call MD: excessive sedation, difficulty breathing, uncontrolled pain

💊 Cardiovascular (BP / CHF)

▼
Teaching cues:
  • BP meds: take same time daily, do not stop abruptly, watch for dizziness/lightheadedness when standing
  • Diuretics: take in morning if possible, monitor for dehydration, potassium-rich foods if on loop diuretic
  • CHF: daily weights (same time, same clothes, same scale), call MD for 2+ lb gain in 24h or 5+ lb in a week
  • Sodium restriction: typically 2g/day for CHF, read labels, avoid processed foods
  • When to call MD: swelling, SOB, weight gain, dizziness, fatigue

💊 General / Other High-Risk Meds

▼
Teaching cues:
  • General: purpose, dose, timing, what to do if a dose is missed, main side effects
  • Steroids: take with food, do not stop abruptly, watch for glucose changes, weight gain, mood changes
  • Thyroid: take on empty stomach, separate from other meds, consistent timing
  • Seizure meds: do not stop abruptly, watch for drowsiness, report changes in seizure frequency
  • When to call MD: new or worsening side effects, missed doses, condition changes
